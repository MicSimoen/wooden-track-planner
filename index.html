🎲 Random track

Makes a layout from the pieces in your inventory that fits on your floor. Loops are built so they close exactly; switches add side tracks, each finished with a buffer stop or a ramp track if you have one. With bridges on, it can also lift part of a loop over a bridge, or make a figure eight that crosses over itself, as long as there's room for your train underneath (Settings). Press again for a different layout.
